ARCHMIND · Case study

Ten apartments on a Doha plot, from first brief to final design

One residential building followed through fifteen stages. Every plan, section, elevation and quantity on this page is drawn from the same set of dimensions, so the numbers in the bill of quantities trace back to the rooms on the plan.

Drawings are schematic and generated from a single dataset. Planning parameters in stage 04 are working assumptions for this case study and must be replaced with the current municipal rules on a live project. Quantities are takeoffs from the schematic model and are not tender quantities.
